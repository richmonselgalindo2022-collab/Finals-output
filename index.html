THE PERSON BEHIND THE PORTFOLIO

Learning with purpose.

This portfolio is organized around my Probability and Statistics learning journey as a BS Computer Science student. It follows the path from describing data to probability, statistical inference, and relationships between variables.

My goal is to make learning visible through activities, examples, projects, and reflections that show how data can support better decisions.
